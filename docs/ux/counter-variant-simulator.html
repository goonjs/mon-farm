Hold it.
Don't tap it.

Tap-target and swipe both failed hands-on testing — too easy to trigger without meaning to, and neither let the operator see a count coming before it landed. This prototypes what replaced them: press-and-hold for three seconds, watched the whole time it's arming. The volume key stays as the one exception — a single press still counts, but a second press inside three seconds is blocked, out loud, not just quietly ignored.

primary method — fixed

V6 Press & hold — 3.0 s

Release before the ring closes and nothing counts — logged as released early, not as an error. A stray brush essentially never survives three full seconds, so the whole stage can be the target.

secondary input — optional

A browser can't read a phone's real volume button — that needs a native wrapper (F‑9). Focus the stage and press ↑ / ↓, or click the rocker. One press counts; press again within 3 s and it's blocked, with a message saying why.
