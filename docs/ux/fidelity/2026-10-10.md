# Design fidelity audit — 2026-10-10

Owner ask (2026-10-10, via the lead): "make sure the app we are doing will be exactly like the MVP design".
This compares the **roll-up** (`chore/rollup-preview-2`, PR #69, head 7c94793) with the owner-approved
mockups `docs/ux/mockups/flow-farm-v1.1.0.html` (36 frames) and `flow-sale-v1.1.0.html` (34 frames). Colour
comes from `docs/color-tokens` (#68), which is included in the roll-up.

## How it was made

- Every `.phone` frame of both mockups was screenshotted. Each of 29 app routes was screenshotted at **390×844 phone
  portrait**, seeded with 3 people, a farm profile, a closed worked-example sale and an open sale.
- Each frame is paired with the nearest app screen: `2026-10-10/pairs/<farm|sale>-NN.png`, mockup on the LEFT and app on the
  RIGHT. `pairs/index.json` lists each pair's caption and route.
- Two reviewers (separate agents) compared every pair. Their rows are below unchanged, apart from the **Check** column
  added here.

## Read this before dispatching

- **Check = capture?** means the app screen captured was not the right state, so the gap is **unverified**:
  - forms and detail screens that need a tap to reach;
  - empty lists, because no seed data was available for that list;
  - `show-account` opened without a sale, which shows its error state;
  - only the **first 844 px** of each screen, because content below scrolls inside the page. On settlement this may hide E05's rounding and accounts sections.

  Verify these rows in the preview before fixing.
- **E04 is not in the roll-up yet.** `feature/e04-sale-2` replaces #64. Most counting-flow rows (sale-03…20) compare
  against the old counter. The reviewer marked what E04 should cover ("E04 pending") and what nothing covers yet
  (s06 partial/bridge baskets, s10 backfill).
- **E02 login/groups is not in the roll-up** (see #65/#69). That leaves the sync pill, the group name and the
  group switch without their backing data, but the visual chrome is still missing.
